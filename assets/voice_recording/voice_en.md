# Voice Trigger Words

The system automatically recognizes and categorizes events based on the following keywords. Simply mention these words in your description to classify the event.

---

### :pan_tool: **Disengagement**
**Keywords**: Takeover, Safety Takeover, Wrong Route, Stuck, Green Light No Go

---

### :gavel: **Violation**
**Keywords**: Violation, Solid Line Crossing, Wrong Lane, Running Red Light

---

### :sentiment_dissatisfied: **Experience**
**Keywords**: Experience, Phantom Braking, Wobble, Too Fast, Too Slow, Unnatural

---

### :stars: **Manual Recording**
**Description**: If your voice description does not contain any of the keywords above, it will be categorized as a "**Manual Mark**" by default.
